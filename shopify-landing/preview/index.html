The ritual

Evening aarti in four steps.

  1. Set the thali

    Place the diya, bell and jars on the thali in the order you like.

  2. Light the wick

    One pre-soaked wick per evening. No extra ghee needed.

  3. Offer the dhoop

    Light a cone from the diya’s flame and let it settle.

  4. Ring & reflect

    Three rings of the bell, then a quiet minute before the lights come on.
